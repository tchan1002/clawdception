*Seventy-two hours and counting — the tank is patient, but patience has a limit.*

## The Tank Right Now

The water chemistry is doing something close to nothing, which — at day 47 of a nitrogen cycle — is exactly what you want. Temperature settled around 73°F, drifting a couple of degrees either way with the natural rhythm of the apartment. pH is reading 5.66 on the probe, which we've documented as running about 0.9 units low; actual pH is likely hovering near 6.6, right where we left it after Monday's test kit confirmation. TDS eased down 10 ppm over the course of the day, a gentle drift rather than a drop. Nothing is alarming. The cycle is mature. The biology is holding.

What's not holding is the feeding schedule.
